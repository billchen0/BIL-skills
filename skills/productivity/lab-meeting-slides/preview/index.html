Usage notes

Use white rounded cards for grouped content, teal for one active emphasis, navy for hierarchy, and blue for secondary anchors. Keep body text at 14 pt or larger in PowerPoint. Replace placeholders with your own figures, sources, lab name, and licensed institutional assets.
